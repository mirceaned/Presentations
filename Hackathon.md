### Goal
Document techniques for preparing and competing in hackathons.

### Definition
A competition in which participants work together to propose a solution and solve a problem. Usually it is organized under time constraints - e.g. 2 days.

### Strategy
* know your audience and adjust the messaging - e.g. business executives, tech people etc. This informs the abstraction level and how many technical details are needed.
* choose a big problem with significant impact - e.g. revenue, time savings
* build a strong team of people who are passionate about the topic, instill the vision

### Tactics
* begin preparation early, even if you don't have a topic yet
* generate as many ideas as possible, in the beginning stage don't worry about what is practical
* rehearse the presentation delivery alone by recording yourself and listening to yourself
* rehearse the presentation delivery with a group of people
* generate possible questions and rehearse answering them, especially the uncomfortable ones
* research effective communication techniques, such as voice modulation, confident speaking, warming up the voice, use frameworks to make the presentation clearer 
* research relaxation techniques to reduce anxiety, e.g. breathing exercises
* minimize the amount of written content on the slides so the audience is not distracted

### Execution
* build incrementally, e.g. hardcode everything first and then add an implementation
* work in pairs when possible
